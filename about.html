About Next Level

Established in Sandton, Johannesburg, we are South Africa's premier high-fidelity gaming sanctuary designed to cultivate next-generation gaming and esports mastery.

Established 2024

Our Founding Story

In early 2024, a team of passionate esports players and high-tech operators realised Sandton was missing a critical piece of infrastructure: a world-class venue dedicated to zero-compromise, competitive-grade gaming. We built Next Level to bridge the gap between casual home setups and pro-standard LAN environments.

Our Mission

To empower South African gamers with uncompromising high-performance rigs, professional coaching frameworks, and a community space built on competitive integrity and comfort.

Our Vision

To position Johannesburg as the beating heart of competitive esports on the continent, producing world-class players backed by top-tier analytical gaming infrastructure.

What Sets Us Apart

10Gbps dedicated fibre, RTX 4090 rigs across the floor, five-star in-house catering, and fully soundproofed private booths for corporate and VIP bookings.
