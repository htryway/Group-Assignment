Our Story

How Next Level Gaming came to be

Next Level Gaming was founded in 2023 by Jason Naidoo, a lifelong competitive gamer who saw a gap in Johannesburg's scene: nowhere brought together elite hardware, real coaching, and a proper stage for local tournaments under one roof. What started as a single row of PCs in a rented warehouse space has grown into a full esports arena — high-spec gaming rigs, private VIP booths, a dedicated training room, and a main stage that's hosted hundreds of matches.

Today, Next Level Gaming is where casual players, competitive teams, schools and corporates come to play, train and celebrate. Whether you're here for a weekend ladder match or a full birthday takeover, the goal has stayed the same since day one: give South African gamers a space worthy of how seriously they take the game.
