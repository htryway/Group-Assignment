Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before you book.

We recommend booking at least 24 hours ahead for solo sessions, and 3–5 days ahead for VIP booths or group packages so we can prepare the space and any add-ons.

Cancellations made more than 24 hours before your session are fully refundable. Cancellations within 24 hours are non-refundable, but can usually be rescheduled once at no extra cost.

All ages are welcome. Visitors under 12 must be accompanied by an adult, and our Esports Training sessions are recommended for ages 10 and up.

Outside food and drinks aren't permitted in the Gaming Zone, but VIP and Birthday Party packages include catering. You're welcome to bring your own mouse, keyboard or controller.

Booking more sessions together automatically lowers your rate: 2 bookings save 5%, 3 save 10%, and 4 or more save 15% off the combined subtotal. Try it on our fee calculator.

Head to the Tournaments page and hit Register on the event you want to enter. Slots are first-come, first-served, and fill up fast for the Weekly Ladder.

We're open Monday–Thursday 10am–10pm, Friday–Saturday 9am–midnight, and Sunday 11am–8pm. Full address and directions are on our Contact page.
